Villa Camila

Upper Eastside, Miami, Florida

Location

Architect

Installation

Category

Located in Miami’s Upper Eastside, Villa Camila blends historic character with modern design. Crystal Box Group partnered with Blanco Design to deliver custom impact windows and doors tailored to the home’s aesthetic and performance needs.

The project included a three-panel entry door, fixed windows, casements, and ES 8000 storefront systems, all finished in dark grey with protective film and pre-glazed for streamlined installation. Systems were specified for impact resistance, UV protection, acoustic control, and energy efficiency, with U-factor certification and multipoint locking where required.
